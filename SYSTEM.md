# System — UNL

Goal: Help a visitor explain their car and coverage needs to an advisor, who quotes personally in LINE chat.

Who: Visitors want a clear path to advice and a real quote. Advisors need enough context to respond without repeated questions. The owner needs to know whether conversations become useful quotes.

Stocks and flows: Visitors arrive, pick their car (brand, model, year), and open LINE with a prepared message. Advisor conversations accumulate until quoted or closed. Unanswered conversations and stale sample packages are liabilities.

Loops: Clear context can shorten advisor response and improve completed enquiries. Extra selection steps can reduce the number of visitors who reach LINE. The website records link clicks, but only the LINE team can confirm actual conversations and quotes.

Delays: A LINE click may not become a sent message. Quote availability and advisor response happen after the visitor leaves the site.

Lever: Goal and information flow. Make advisor contact the outcome of the journey; do not require choosing a sample insurance package.

Delete: Public sample package ranking, comparison, and package detail as required steps. Keep purchase and admin code inactive until Core decides its disposition.

Watch: Mobile completion from car selection to LINE click; advisor-confirmed conversations and quotes; response delay; whether visitors arrive with usable car and needs context.
