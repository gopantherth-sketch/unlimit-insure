# Verify Before Launch

Claims in customer-facing copy that a licensed person / business owner must confirm. Source: `verify` fields in `content/*.ts` (search `verify:`).

## Business claims (homepage)
- "เปรียบเทียบหลายบริษัท" — authorised insurer list.
- "ช่วยเหลือฉุกเฉิน 24 ชม." — which plans include it.
- After-sales / advisor support scope actually offered.
- Broker licence number (Trust strip placeholder).

## Policy types
- ชั้น 1 generally covers no-counterparty collision, fire, theft, third parties.
- Flood included in many but not all ชั้น 1; availability on 2+/others or as add-on.
- ชั้น 2+/3+ collision cover requires an identifiable land-vehicle counterparty; definition of "คู่กรณี".
- ชั้น 2+ generally includes fire/theft; 3+ and 3 generally do not.
- ชั้น 3 covers third-party liability only.

## Sum insured & excess
- Sum insured set near market value, decreasing with age.
- Theft / total loss paid up to sum insured; depreciation and salvage treatment.
- Cases where an excess applies without being chosen (no counterparty, at fault) and amounts under current wording.
- Excess applied per incident.

## Repair & riders
- Dealer repair age limits by insurer.
- Rider codes ร.ย.01 / 02 / 03.
- Voluntary cover pays above พ.ร.บ.; medical cover regardless of fault.

## Flood / theft / EV
- Exclusion for deliberately driving into floodwater; "don't start the engine" advice.
- Theft claim documents (police report etc.).
- EV policy wording, battery depreciation, wear exclusion, charger/cable cover scope.

## พ.ร.บ.
- Legal requirement and link to annual tax renewal; covers death/injury only; people inside and outside the car. No statutory amounts stated in copy.

## Process
- Price confirmation and vehicle inspection before issuance.
- Plans purchasable fully online; payment channels.
- Instalment terms.
