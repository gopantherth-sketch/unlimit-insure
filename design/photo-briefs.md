# Photo Briefs

Prompts for the homepage and Lab photo slots (`components/brand/Photo.tsx`). Deliver JPG or PNG at the **minimum size or larger** in the **stated ratio**; the PM crops, compresses to .webp (≤ 250 KB) and drops them into `public/images/`.

Rules for every image:
- No car brand badges, logos or emblems; no readable number plates (blank or blurred plate).
- No text, watermarks or UI in the image.
- Real-looking photography, bright natural daylight, clean and modern; colour palette leaning white / silver / soft sky blue so it sits well on the blue-white site.
- People: Thai / Southeast Asian, natural smiles, not posed-looking stock. If AI-generated, check hands, teeth, eyes and car details (wheels, mirrors, wipers) before sending.

Negative prompt (add to every prompt if your tool supports it):
`logo, brand emblem, badge, text, watermark, number plate, license plate characters, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed wheels, cartoon, illustration, 3d render, oversaturated, dark, night, rain`

---

## 1. Hero car — `hero-suv.webp`
- Ratio **7:5 landscape** (e.g. 2100 × 1500 minimum 1400 × 1000)
- Composition: car on the **right two-thirds**, front three-quarter view facing left-toward-camera; **empty soft sky on the left third** (headline sits there on desktop). Car fully in frame with some road visible under it.

Prompt:
> Professional automotive photograph of a modern silver-white compact crossover SUV, front three-quarter view, parked on a clean empty city road, bright late-morning daylight, soft clear light-blue sky, blurred modern glass skyscrapers of Bangkok in the background, shallow depth of field, crisp reflections on the paint, generous empty sky on the left side of the frame, clean minimal commercial style, high detail, no logos, no badges, blank number plate

## 2. Lifestyle driver — `lifestyle-driver.webp`
- Ratio **14:9 landscape** (e.g. 1800 × 1157, minimum 1200 × 771)
- Composition: woman and car window on the **left half**; right half softer / out of focus (text sits on the right on desktop).

Prompt:
> Candid lifestyle photograph of a smiling young Thai woman in her late 20s sitting in the driver's seat of a silver car, arm resting on the open window, looking out happily toward the camera's right, bright natural daylight, soft green and sky-blue background out of focus, warm natural skin tones, casual smart clothing, shallow depth of field, subject on the left half of the frame, fresh optimistic mood, no logos

## 3. Advisor — `advisor.webp`

Prompt:
